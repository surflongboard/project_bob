Read before sharing this further
§Every photo here is a screenshot of a live haglofs.com (or third-party retailer) product page, cropped to just the garment on a neutral backdrop — no browser chrome, price, or buttons. It's still someone else's product photography, not licensed imagery, so treat it as a review-mechanic test, not something to publish externally as-is.
§7 of 34 photographed cards carry a caveat — 6 show a "II"/successor generation rather than the exact SKU the sales figure is attributed to, 1 has an uncertain size/variant match. See the "Known issues" table at the bottom of the page for the full list with jump links back up to each card — nearly a fifth of this photographed set being a successor is a settled fact about how haglofs.com's live site works (it shows the current generation regardless of which one a sales figure belongs to), not a per-item curiosity. Decide once whether a successor photo is an acceptable stand-in before scaling this to the full portfolio.
§Keep/Merge/Update choices are saved in this browser only (not shared with anyone else who opens this page, and not synced anywhere) — a quick way to test the interaction, not a real review record yet.
§FY25 and YTD26 Sales/GM%/GP figures are the published, audited Sheet-2 basis — same numbers as the tiering workbook and Tier Bench. GP (Gross Profit, SEK) = Sales × GM%, not its own workbook column. FY25 Units (added REG-020) are real but raw-recomputed from source exports rather than an audited published total — see the register for the caveat.
§The small badges under each tier chip (Nordic / ROW = Rest of World, i.e. Non-Nordic) show that franchise's REGION-RELATIVE tier (REG-023/024) — each region ranked against its own population, with SEK floors scaled to that region's share of FY25 sales, not Global's unscaled floors. A muted (non-highlighted) badge means this franchise is a Global Hero but is NOT a Hero within that region alone — that's the intended effect of region-relative thresholds, not a data error.
